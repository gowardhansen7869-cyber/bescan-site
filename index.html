Features

Every document tool, in one app

BeScan bundles a complete document toolkit so you never need to juggle multiple scanning apps.

Document Scanner

Capture crisp, auto-cropped scans of paperwork, receipts and notes in seconds.

OCR Text Recognition

Turn scanned pages into selectable, searchable and copyable text instantly.

Image to PDF

Convert any photo or gallery image into a clean, shareable PDF file.

Merge PDF

Combine multiple PDF files into a single, organized document.

Split PDF

Break large PDFs into separate pages or custom page ranges.

Compress PDF

Shrink file sizes for easy sharing without losing readability.

QR Scanner

Read QR codes instantly using your camera, online or offline.

QR Generator

Create custom QR codes for links, text, contacts and more.

eSign

Sign PDF documents right on your phone with a personal signature.

Watermark

Stamp text or logo watermarks onto your PDFs to protect ownership.
