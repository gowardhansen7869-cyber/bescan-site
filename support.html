Report a Bug or Request a Feature

Help us make BeScan better by sharing what you find.

1

Describe the issue

Note what you were doing in the app, what you expected, and what happened instead.

2

Add your device details

Include your Android version, device model and the BeScan app version from your Play Store update history.

3

Attach a screenshot

A screenshot or screen recording helps us understand and reproduce the issue faster.

4

Send it to support

Email everything to bescan.support@gmail.com and we'll follow up.

Feature Requests

Have an idea for a tool or improvement you'd like to see in BeScan? We read every suggestion. Send your idea to bescan.support@gmail.com with a short explanation of the problem it would solve — the more concrete the use case, the easier it is for us to prioritize.
