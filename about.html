About Us

Building simple, secure Android apps

BeScan is developed and maintained by CG Apps Dev, a developer focused on practical, privacy-respecting productivity tools.

Our Story

BeScan started from a straightforward observation: most people need to scan a document, sign a form, or shrink a PDF far more often than they need a bloated office suite. CG Apps Dev built BeScan to handle exactly those everyday moments — quickly, reliably, and without demanding an account or an internet connection.

Every tool inside BeScan, from the scanner to the QR generator, is designed to work end-to-end on the device in your hand, so your documents never have to leave your phone to be processed.

Our Mission

Create simple, secure and privacy-focused Android applications that respect the person using them as much as the task they came to finish.

Our Vision

Provide reliable productivity applications for everyone — regardless of technical skill, connectivity, or budget.

What Guides Our Development

  • Privacy by default — no unnecessary permissions, no forced sign-in.
  • On-device processing wherever technically possible.
  • A clean, Material-inspired interface that stays easy to navigate.
  • Honest, transparent communication about data and permissions.
  • Continuous improvement driven by real user feedback.
